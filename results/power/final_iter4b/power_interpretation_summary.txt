Interpretation
--------------

Power increases as the interpolation factor L increases.

The higher interpolation factors generate more valid output-sample events per
input interval, increasing switching activity in the active DSP datapath and
especially in the valid-driven FIR processing.

The final reported project power result for the highest-rate L5 configuration
is:

  40.3975 mW whole-chip
  6.2380 mW DSP core
